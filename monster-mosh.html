How to play

Survive the mosh pit for 10 minutes, then defeat the Mosh King. Your abilities fire on their own: you handle the moving.


WASD move   Mouse look (click to lock)   Space jump   Shift dash-slide (brief invincibility)   Esc pause   Wheel zoom

Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb drags the camera, buttons jump and dash.


💎 Kill monsters to drop XP gems. Each level-up offers cards: new abilities, upgrades, or passives. You can hold 4 abilities and 4 passives. Get an ability to level 5 to EVOLVE it.


⭐ Glowing elites hit harder and drop loot. A mini-boss crashes in at 5:00. Telegraphed attacks show red on the ground: get out, or jump over shockwaves.


🪙 Coins unlock new monsters and permanent upgrades.
