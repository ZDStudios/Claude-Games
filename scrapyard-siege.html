HOW TO PLAY

Build

  • Pick a tower card in the Scrap Shop, then click a tile beside the path.
  • Click a tower to upgrade it (5 upgrades each), change its targeting or sell it.
  • Maxed towers can be overclocked forever for more power.
  • Rally Bots buff nearby towers. Scrap Mints pay out every round.
  • Torpedo Trout fish swim backwards down the path, ramming and shoving robots.
  • Your game saves automatically into one of 3 slots. Reloading mid-round restarts that round.
  • Stealth bots need detection: Longshot, Flak, Piercer and Rocket upgrades, Rally Bot's Radar Dish, or Frosty's Snow Radar.

The Director

  • A hidden AI scans your defence before every round and builds a wave just for you.
  • It learns from each round and remembers you between sessions.
  • Expect breathers, build-ups, spikes and a boss every 10 rounds.

Controls

  • 1–0 - = Z X C V select towers   Esc/right-click cancel
  • Shift+click to keep placing
  • Space start round   F speed   P pause
  • U upgrade   Del sell   T targeting
  • Right-drag or Q/E orbit the camera, mouse wheel zooms
  • M sound   N music
